# Fixed Operations – Consultative Model (GST)

## Purpose

Provide a structured, repeatable framework for evaluating Fixed Operations performance across staffing, process, traffic, execution, retention, and profitability.

## 1. Dealership Staffing

### Intent

Ensure staffing levels, skill mix, and compensation structures support volume, efficiency, and profitability targets.

### Review Areas

- Organizational structure and role clarity
- Hiring, onboarding, training, and succession planning
- Pay plans aligned to desired behaviors

### Key Questions

- Are staffing counts aligned with performance targets?
- Are job descriptions current and outcome-driven?
- Are pay plans aligned across departments?
- Are training and certifications current or planned?
- Is there a coaching, mentoring, and recognition process?

### KPIs / Metrics

- RO per ASM
- RO per Technician
- Technician efficiency and utilization
- Turnover rates
- Certification completion

### Health Signals

- Balanced ASM and technician ratios
- High efficiency with low turnover
- Clear succession planning

### Risk Signals

- Overloaded ASMs or techs
- High turnover
- Incomplete certifications

## 2. Dealership Processes

### Intent

Ensure consistent, documented, and followed service processes from appointment to delivery.

### Review Areas

- Appointment scheduling and BDC alignment
- Write-up, walk-around, menu selling
- Communication, follow-up, and delivery

### KPIs / Metrics

- Appointment show rate
- CXI comments
- Google reviews

### Health Signals

- Documented SOPs followed consistently
- Positive CXI sentiment

### Risk Signals

- Process drift
- Inconsistent training

## 3. Traffic Generation

### Intent

Drive sufficient fixed ops traffic aligned to capacity and growth objectives.

### Review Areas

- BDC appointment setting
- Online scheduling usability
- Marketing alignment to service growth

### KPIs / Metrics

- Appointment conversion rate
- Show rate
- ToyotaCare and Service Connect redemption

### Health Signals

- Strong appointment-to-show conversion
- Scheduler aligned to capacity

### Risk Signals

- Blackout scheduling
- Poor phone/email capture

## 4. Reception / MPI

### Intent

Maximize parts and labor sales through consistent MPI execution and presentation.

### Review Areas

- MPI completion and presentation process
- Video MPI usage
- Parts penetration

### KPIs / Metrics

- MPI completion rate
- MPI sold rate
- Parts dollars per RO

### Health Signals

- High MPI completion and close rates
- Strong parts-to-labor ratio

### Risk Signals

- Incomplete or late MPI delivery
- Missed upsell opportunities

## 5. Repair Flow

### Intent

Optimize RO flow, technician productivity, and quality.

### Review Areas

- Dispatching model
- Parts-to-stall flow
- Stall utilization

### KPIs / Metrics

- Hours per RO
- Stall utilization
- Comebacks / Fix-it-right

### Health Signals

- Smooth dispatch and minimal waiting
- Low comeback rates

### Risk Signals

- Bottlenecks
- Parts delays

## 6. Customer Retention

### Intent

Maximize long-term profitability through repeat service visits.

### Review Areas

- ToyotaCare and Connected Services usage
- CXI analysis beyond scores

### KPIs / Metrics

- PMA return rate
- CXI sentiment
- Redemption rates

### Health Signals

- Strong PMA retention
- Active use of CXI insights

### Risk Signals

- Declining return rates
- Ignored customer feedback

## 7. Parts & Collision

### Intent

Ensure parts and collision operations support fixed ops throughput and profitability.

### Review Areas

- Inventory management
- Lost sales tracking
- Tool utilization

### KPIs / Metrics

- Parts-to-labor ratio
- Inventory turn
- Lost sales rate

### Health Signals

- High fill rates
- Controlled inventory aging

### Risk Signals

- Excess / obsolete inventory
- Poor lost-sales processes

## 8. Profit Retention

### Intent

Protect and grow fixed ops profitability.

### Review Areas

- Expense control
- Gross profit by department
- Service absorption

### KPIs / Metrics

- Service absorption
- Gross by department
- Return on sales

### Health Signals

- Strong absorption ratios
- Controlled expenses

### Risk Signals

- Margin erosion
- Misaligned pay plans
